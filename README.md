# Brixwall

**Your containers, built into a living brick wall.**

Brixwall turns the containers running on your computer into a bright, pixel-art game world. Every container becomes a colourful brick in a wall. As your apps start, stop and recover, the wall changes with them, live, right in front of you.

No dashboards full of tables. No terminal commands to remember. Just open Brixwall and see, at a glance, how everything on your machine is doing.

---

## How it feels

Picture a sunny side-scrolling level: blue sky, drifting clouds, grassy ledges, and a stone wall waiting to be filled.

- **A new container starts** and a crate floats down on a parachute, then drops neatly into its place in the wall.
- **A container is healthy** and its brick shows a small green light.
- **A container is starting up** and the light turns amber while it gets ready.
- **A container is struggling** and the light turns red, so you notice straight away.
- **A container stops** and its brick turns grey.
- **A container is removed** and its brick tumbles out of the wall.
- **Everything is healthy** and the flag at the end of the level rises.

A little dock worker in a yellow hard hat walks along the ground in front of the wall. You can move them around and jump, just for fun.

---

## What you can do

- **Watch your wall build itself** as your containers come to life.
- **Track your progress** with a counter that shows how many containers are healthy out of the total.
- **Click any brick** to see what that container is, what it runs, and how long it has been up.
- **Read a container's recent logs** without leaving the page.
- **Know when your container engine is off.** Brixwall tells you plainly instead of showing an empty screen.

---

## Who it is for

- Developers who run several services on their machine and want a friendly view of what is up and what is down.
- Teams who want something fun on a spare screen during a demo or a hack day.
- Anyone learning about containers who finds a picture easier to follow than a list.

---

## Private by design

Brixwall runs only on your own computer. It does not send anything anywhere, it has no accounts, and it keeps nothing once you close it. In this version it only looks at your containers and never changes them.

Because it can see everything your container engine sees, Brixwall is meant to stay on your machine. Please do not put it on the public internet.

---

## Try it with sample containers

Brixwall comes with a small kit that starts a set of harmless sample containers one by one, so you can watch the wall fill up. The kit can also switch random sample containers on and off to show the wall reacting, then tidy everything away when you are done. It only ever touches the sample containers it created, never your own.
